# PayMinute brand

- **Look:** mobile layout with white cards on a light grey background (`#f1f3f4`), near-black text (`#111111`), blue actions (`#1a73e8`), a bottom tab bar and a bottom-sheet payment flow.
- **Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans, regular weight everywhere (no bold).
- **Avatars:** the first letter of the person's name on a colour unique to that person (hashed from their email or name).
- **Logo:** shield + check mark (`payminute-logo.svg`). Canva design: https://canva.link/ifsizr62xqqfzbb
- PayMinute uses its own name, colours and logo.
